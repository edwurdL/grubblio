Privacy Policy

Grubbl.io · Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version: you can play without giving us anything. If you make an account, we keep your name, email, password (hashed) and the games you play, only to run your account. We don't show ads, use analytics or tracking cookies, or sell or share your data for marketing. You can delete your account, and everything in it, at any time from My games → Account settings.

Who we are

Grubbl.io (“Grubblio”, “we”) is a free food-guessing game at grubbl.io. For anything about your data, email privacy@grubbl.io.

If you play without an account

  • On your device: your browser's local storage keeps your current games (puzzle and guesses), your sound and volume settings, and whether this browser has signed in before. This stays on your device; clearing your browser data removes it. We don't set cookies for players without an account.
  • On our server: to score each guess, your browser sends us the puzzle and the dishes you've guessed. We don't store these for players without an account. Like any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes your IP address and basic request details (such as browser type) to deliver the site, protect it from abuse, and keep short-lived technical logs.

If you create an account

We store:

  • Your name and email address. You confirm the email with a one-time code before the account is created.
  • Your password, stored only as a secure hash. We can't see the password itself.
  • Your game history: for each game you guess in while signed in, the mode (Daily dish or Rush hour), the answer, your guesses, the result, and when you played. It powers My games, your stats and streaks, and lets you continue an unfinished Rush hour on another device.
  • Sign-in sessions: one record per device you're signed in on, with its IP address and browser details, and a session cookie on that device that keeps you signed in. This cookie is needed for the account to work; it isn't used for tracking.
  • One-time codes for sign-up and password resets, stored hashed and expiring after 10 minutes.
  • Abuse-prevention counters, keyed by IP address, that limit how often codes can be requested and sign-ins attempted.

Why we use it

  • To provide the account you asked for: signing you in, saving your games and showing your stats.
  • To send the codes that verify your email and reset your password. We don't send marketing emails.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse, such as automated sign-ups or password guessing.

If you're in the EU or UK, our legal bases are performing our agreement with you (running your account) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Who else handles data

We use a few service providers, only to run the game:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site, stores the database, and processes requests and logs. If bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile) is turned on, it checks the sign-up and password-reset forms for automated traffic.
  • Resend delivers our emails, so it receives your email address and the code we're sending.
  • Google Fonts provides the game's fonts. Your browser downloads them from Google, which receives your IP address when it does.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising, and we don't use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

  • Account details and game history: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: until you sign out, or 60 days after last use.
  • One-time codes: 10 minutes, or until used.
  • Technical logs and abuse-prevention counters: a short time, then deleted or overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your name or password under My games → Account settings.
  • Delete your account there too. It immediately removes your account, sessions and game history.
  • Other requests, such as a copy of your data or a correction, can go to privacy@grubbl.io. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Grubbl.io isn't directed at children under 13, and you shouldn't create an account if you're under 13 (or the minimum age where you live). If we learn that a child has created an account, we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
